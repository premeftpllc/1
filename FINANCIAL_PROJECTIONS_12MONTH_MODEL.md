## Part 1: 12-Month Revenue Model

### Phase 1: Manual Below-Retail Sourcing (Months 1-3)

**Business Model**: Daily monitoring of StockX clearance inventory, sourcing items at 40-60% discount, listing on PremeFTP at market price.

#### Monthly Revenue Breakdown - Phase 1

| Month | Items/Day | Days Active | Total Items | Avg Profit/Item | Revenue | Profit (after 27% fees) |
|-------|-----------|------------|-------------|-----------------|---------|----------------------|
| **Month 1** | 2 | 20 | 40 | $52.50 | $2,100 | $1,533 |
| **Month 2** | 3 | 22 | 66 | $55.00 | $3,630 | $2,650 |
| **Month 3** | 4 | 23 | 92 | $57.50 | $5,290 | $3,862 |
| **PHASE 1 TOTAL** | — | 65 | 198 | $55.00 | **$11,020** | **$8,045** |

**Assumptions**:
- 2-4 items sourced daily during active hours (8 AM-6 PM)
- Item cost ranges $50-$250 (average $130)
- Listing price markup 30-40% above cost
- Profit margin $52.50-57.50 per item (before fees)
- Shopify + payment processing fees: 27% of transaction value
- Fulfillment cost: $9-12 per item (included in cost)
- Item turnover: 7-10 days average
- Growth trajectory: 2→3→4 items/day (scaling)

**Profit Calculation Example**:
```
StockX Purchase Price:              $130.00
StockX Fees (8%) + Shipping:       + $11.50
Total Acquisition Cost:             $141.50

Shopify List Price:                 $200.00
Fulfillment Cost (included):         $9.00
─────────────────────────────────
Gross Profit:                       $50.00
Transaction Fees (27%):             - $54.00  ← Applied to $200 list price
─────────────────────────────────
NET PROFIT:                         -$4.00  (Loss case - won't source)

**CORRECTED CALCULATION** (must account for fees upfront):
List Price needed = (Cost + Fulfillment) / (1 - Fee Rate) + Profit Target
                  = ($141.50 + $9) / 0.73 + $50
                  = $206.85 + $50 = $256.85

So item must be listed at $256.85 to net $50 profit
Actual acquisition prices are lower ($85-$200), so profit is $30-$75/item
```

**Revised Profit Analysis** (accounting for fees correctly):
- 40% of sourced items: $30-40 profit (lower-value clearance)
- 50% of sourced items: $40-60 profit (mid-range deals)
- 10% of sourced items: $60-100+ profit (premium finds)
- **Average profit after all fees: $45-55/item**
