## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates four leading fashion resale platforms for arbitrage opportunities, identifying pricing gaps, fee structures, and profit potential. Key findings:

- **Most favorable fee structure:** Mercari (10% flat) and Grailed (9% base)
- **Highest margins:** Poshmark for designer luxury ($500+) with authentication

## PART 1: PLATFORM ANALYSIS

### 1. DEPOP ANALYSIS

#### Fee Structure (2026)
- **Selling Commission:** $0 (removed July 2024 for US sellers)
- **Payment Processing:** 3.3% + $0.45 per transaction
- **Effective rate on $50 sale:** 4.2% ($2.10)
- **Optional Boosts:** +12% when listing is boosted and sells
- **International (non-US/UK):** Still charges 10% selling fee

**Fee Breakdown:**
```
$50 sale → $50 × 3.3% + $0.45 = $2.10 fees → $47.90 payout
$100 sale → $100 × 3.3% + $0.45 = $3.75 fees → $96.25 payout
$200 sale → $200 × 3.3% + $0.45 = $7.05 fees → $192.95 payout
```

#### Platform Characteristics
- **Core audience:** Gen-Z (aged 13-35), primarily female-skewing (70% of users)
- **Top categories:** Adidas Sambas, Doc Martens, customized Air Force 1s, vintage tees
- **Market strength:** Casual streetwear, vintage fashion, customized items
- **Search visibility:** Algorithm favors recent activity; frequent listers get higher visibility
- **Authentication:** No mandatory authentication system; seller reputation paramount

#### Competitive Advantages
- **Lowest effective fees** among major fashion platforms
- Strong mobile-first experience (90% of sales via app)
- Built-in social features (followers, activity feeds)
- Rapid transaction velocity for viral items
- Cross-sell opportunity with TikTok trends

#### Market Demand Patterns
- **Trend velocity:** 3-7 day trend cycles
- **Best sellers:** Vintage band tees ($15-50), customized sneakers ($60-150), retro sportswear
- **Seasonal peaks:** Q4 (Nov-Dec), back-to-school (Aug-Sep)
- **Price sensitivity:** High; buyers expect negotiation

#### Supreme/Streetwear Pricing Context
- Supreme apparel sales +19% YoY in H1 2026
- Average price premiums on Supreme jumped from 11% to 41% (StockX data)
- Depop customers often price 10-20% below StockX authenticated rates
- Box logo tees: $80-250 (Depop) vs. $150-400 (StockX)

### 2. MERCARI ANALYSIS

#### Fee Structure (2026)
- **Selling Commission:** 10% flat on item price + buyer-paid shipping
- **Payment Processing Fee:** Eliminated (effective Jan 6, 2026)
- **Shipping Weight Correction Fee:** 8% on adjusted shipping (if over-estimated)
- **Buyer Protection Fee:** 3.6% charged to buyer (doesn't come from seller payout)

**Fee Breakdown:**
```
$50 sale (free shipping) → $50 × 10% = $5.00 fees → $45.00 payout
$100 sale ($10 shipping) → $110 × 10% = $11.00 fees → $99.00 payout
$200 sale (free shipping) → $200 × 10% = $20.00 fees → $180.00 payout
```

#### Platform Characteristics
- **Core audience:** Broad demographics; strong mobile adoption
- **Top categories:** Collectibles (trading cards, action figures), electronics, fashion vintage
- **Market strength:** Collectibles resale, bulk lots, price-sensitive buyers
- **Search visibility:** Category-based sorting; "new" tag for recent listings
- **Authentication:** Platform-authenticated badges for high-value items

#### Competitive Advantages
- **Lowest commission rate** among fashion resale platforms (10% flat)
- Strong collectibles market (trading cards, vintage toys, memorabilia)
- Efficient bulk listing capability
- Lower buyer expectations on price negotiation
- Good for commodity items and high-volume sellers

#### Market Demand Patterns
- **Trend velocity:** 2-4 week cycles
- **Best sellers:** Sports cards, vintage collectibles ($50-500), bulk clothing lots ($100-300)
- **Seasonal peaks:** Q4 (collectibles), Q2-Q3 (summer clearance)
- **Price sensitivity:** Medium; less negotiation-driven than Depop/Poshmark

#### Collectibles Market Specifics
- Trading cards: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, sports cards command premium pricing
- Vintage toys: Action figures, vintage video games show strong velocity
- Fashion bulk lots: T-shirt bundles ($50-150), vintage band tee collections
- Average item value: $30-150 (higher than Depop)

### 3. POSHMARK ANALYSIS

#### Fee Structure (2026)
- **Sales Under $15:** $2.95 flat fee
- **Sales $15 and Above:** 20% commission
- **Shipping:** Flat $7.97 (USPS Ground Advantage, up to 5 lbs, 2-5 business days)
- **Additional Cost:** Seller pays nothing for shipping labels; buyer covers $7.97
- **Posh Authenticate:** FREE for items $500+ (physical inspection by Poshmark team)

**Fee Breakdown:**
```
$15 sale → $15 × 20% = $3.00 fees ($2.95 vs $3.00, use $2.95) → $12.05 payout
$50 sale → $50 × 20% = $10.00 fees → $40.00 payout
$100 sale → $100 × 20% = $20.00 fees → $80.00 payout
$500 sale (luxury) → $500 × 20% = $100.00 fees + Posh Authenticate FREE → $400.00 payout
```

#### Platform Characteristics
- **Core audience:** Female-dominant (85%+), fashion-conscious, 25-55 age range
- **Top categories:** Designer handbags, shoes, apparel, luxury goods
- **Market strength:** Premium fashion, designer bags, authenticated luxury ($500+)
- **Search visibility:** Social selling; closet activity, bundle offers drive visibility
- **Authentication:** Posh Authenticate included free for $500+ items

#### Competitive Advantages
- **Best for luxury ($500+):** Free authentication increases buyer trust dramatically
- **Fastest sales velocity** for designer items (9-hour average on luxury pieces)
- **Bundle/multi-item strategy:** Buyers combine purchases; flat shipping on bundles
- **Buyer premium:** Customers expect higher quality; willing to pay premiums
- **Community engagement:** Social features drive repeat customers

#### Market Demand Patterns
- **Trend velocity:** 1-2 week cycles
- **Best sellers:** Designer bags ($200-1,000), premium footwear ($80-300), apparel bundles ($50-150)
- **Seasonal peaks:** Q4 (holiday), Q1 (New Year's resolutions)
- **Price sensitivity:** Low for designer items; negotiation-friendly for moderate price items

#### Fashion/Apparel Specifics
- **Designer handbags:** Kate Spade, Coach, Michael Kors, Tory Burch command 60-80% of retail
- **Luxury ($500+):** Hermes, Gucci, Prada, Louis Vuitton drive highest margins
- **Premium footwear:** Nike Jordan, Adidas Yeezy, Coach, UGG sell well
- **Apparel:** Sweaters, jackets, jeans (bundle discount of 15-25% typical)

#### Pricing Advantage vs Mercari
- Poshmark items sell 10-20% higher than Mercari equivalents
- $425 Kate Spade satchel: 9 hours on Poshmark vs. 41 days on Mercari
- Premium buyer base justifies higher margins

```
$100 sale → ($100 × 6%) + ($100 × 3.49%) + $0.49 = $11.48 fees → $88.52 payout
$150 sale → ($150 × 9%) + ($150 × 3.49%) + $0.49 = $19.43 fees → $130.57 payout
$300 sale → ($300 × 9%) + ($300 × 3.49%) + $0.49 = $38.36 fees → $261.64 payout
$500 sale → ($500 × 9%) + ($500 × 3.49%) + $0.49 = $65.99 fees → $434.01 payout
```

- **Market strength:** Premium streetwear specialist; authenticated high-ticket items
- **Search visibility:** Tag-based discovery; reputation drives visibility

- **Specialized streetwear audience:** Buyers specifically seeking vintage and designer pieces

#### Streetwear Specialist Insights
- **Vintage Supreme:** $80-250 (lower than StockX but higher than Depop)
- **Rare collaborations:** Comme des Garçons, Undercover, Yohji Yamamoto, Rick Owens
- **Heritage brands:** Levi's vintage (1960s-80s), vintage band tees, workwear
- **Designer sneakers:** Nike SB, Adidas Yeezy, Patta, Jordan collabs

#### Reputation System Mechanics

- Payout = Selling Price - Fees
- Acquisition Cost = Item cost + sourcing labor
- Shipping Cost = USPS/carrier label cost (varies by weight/zone)

### Platform-Specific Risks

**Depop:**
- Account suspension (high-volume sellers)
- Trend dependency (3-7 day cycles require constant activity)
- Buyer disputes over customization/alterations
- Solution: Maintain 4-5 backup accounts, moderate listing velocity, document condition thoroughly

**Mercari:**
- Weight/shipping correction fees (8% on underestimated shipping)
- Bulk listings flagged as spam
- Collectibles authentication disputes
- Solution: Weigh all items before listing, space out bulk uploads, get items pre-authenticated by third-party

**Poshmark:**
- 20% commission (highest among peers) reduces margins
- Bundle discount strategy risk (discounting too aggressively)
- Slow-moving inventory ties up capital
- Solution: Use for premium items only ($100+), set strict discount floors, rotate inventory monthly

**Grailed:**
- Low volume (1-2 sales/month avg) = slow capital turnover
- Reputation system vulnerability (single negative review impacts Trusted Seller status)
- Shipping delays damage reputation
- Solution: Use priority/express shipping, respond to inquiries within 12 hours, pack professionally
