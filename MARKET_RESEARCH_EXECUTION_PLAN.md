### Path 2: Market Evidence Collection (10-15 min per item)
**Objective:** Gather current secondary market pricing and liquidity data

**Required Fields to Complete:**

1. **Current Market Price** (USD)
   - Source: Last 30-day average from StockX/Grailed
   - Method: Average of completed sales, not asking prices

2. **Price Type** (Market Evidence field)
   - Options: Retail, Secondary Market, Auction, Estimate
   - Default: Secondary Market (for resale assessment)

3. **Liquidity Evidence** (Days to Sell, Sales Volume)
   - StockX: Check 30/60-day volume metrics
   - Grailed: Track active listings vs sold in last 30 days
   - eBay: Historical sales velocity

4. **Evidence Quality Score** (1-5)
   - 5: Real-time trading data (StockX, Grailed sold), 100+ sales
   - 4: Recent data (within 7 days), 50-100 sales
   - 3: Moderate data (within 30 days), 20-50 sales
   - 2: Aged data (30-90 days), <20 sales
   - 1: Historical only, no recent market activity

5. **Checked At** (Date/Time)
   - Timestamp of market research (current date)

**Data Sources Priority:**
1. **StockX** - Live pricing + volume (primary)
2. **Grailed** - Active market + bids (secondary)
3. **eBay** - Completed auctions (tertiary)
4. **Supreme resale communities** - Consensus pricing
