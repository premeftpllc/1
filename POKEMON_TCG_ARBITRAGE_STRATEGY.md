## 4. Authentication & Grading Strategy

### 4.1 Counterfeit Detection Framework

#### Critical Authentication Methods

**1. Light Test**
- Hold card to bright light
- **Authentic:** Dark core layer blocks most light
- **Fake:** Cheap paper glows bright (lacks core layer)
- **Limitation:** Only catches most obvious fakes

**2. PSA Slab Verification (for graded cards)**
- Use UV light to check for hidden "PSA" text
- Should glow evenly in two fixed locations
- **Red Flags:** Patchy glow, over-intense areas, incorrect positioning
- Verify QR code on label (pulls matching cert from PSA database)

**3. Print Quality Analysis**
- Font clarity and saturation (modern print: sharp and crisp)
- Holographic pattern consistency (check light reflection)
- Cardstock thickness & weight (modern: 0.30-0.35mm)
- Edge printing alignment (authentic: precise registration)

**4. Watermark & Security Features**
- Modern Pokemon TCG includes microprint watermarks
- Authentic cards: consistent tiny text under magnification
- Fake cards: usually blank or blurry under magnification

#### Red Flag Checklist Before Purchase
- [ ] Seller history (avoid accounts <30 days old or low feedback)
- [ ] Price unrealistically low (<70% of market rate = likely fake)
- [ ] Mint/PSA 10 cards in bulk lots (suspicious clustering)
- [ ] Slabbed cards with defaced/scratched cert numbers
- [ ] Mixed authenticity claims ("possibly authentic")

- **Best for:** Condition-sensitive cards where subgrades matter

- **Market:** More popular in sports card space (less Pokémon preference)
- **ROI:** Similar to PSA but less Pokémon-specific liquidity

### 4.3 Grading ROI Calculation Framework

**Formula:** `(Graded Value × 0.85*) - Raw Value - Grading Cost = Net Profit**  
*0.85 = typical 15% sellthrough discount (not all grade at asking price)

**Example: Chase Rare Card Grading**
- Raw Value: $180
- Estimated PSA 10 Value: $450
- PSA Regular Cost: $100
- Platform Fee (10%): $40
- **Net Profit:** ($450 × 0.85) - $180 - $100 - $40 = **$133 (74% ROI)**
- **Timeline:** 75 days (capital lockup)

**Example: CGC Volume Play (5-card batch)**
- Raw Values: $120, $150, $140, $160, $130 (avg $140)
- CGC Batch Cost: $15 × 5 = $75 total + $40 shipping = $115
- Estimated CGC 8.5+ avg value: $280 avg per card
- Platform Fee (10%): $140
- **Net Profit:** ($280 × 5 × 0.85) - ($140 × 5) - $115 - $140 = **$480 (132% ROI)**
- **Timeline:** 30 days (faster capital cycle)

### 4.4 Grading Risk Factors

**High-Risk Scenarios:**
1. **Centering issues** - Cards with off-center printing grade lower (subjectivity)
2. **Surface wear** - Spots/scratches often drop grade by 1-2 points
3. **Market shifts** - Graded card demand can collapse quickly if meta changes
4. **Grade clustering** - Many cards grade 7-8 (not premium grades), negative ROI
5. **Fake detection by grader** - Cards rejected as counterfeit (total loss)
