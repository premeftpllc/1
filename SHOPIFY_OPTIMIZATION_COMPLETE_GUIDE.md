## 1. Product Title Optimization

### Why Title Optimization Matters
- Titles are the #1 factor in click-through rate (CTR) from search results
- First 60 characters display in search results (mobile: 50 chars)
- Affects both SEO ranking and user decision-making
- Average CTR improvement: 15-25% with optimized titles

### Title Formula Template
```
[Brand] [Item Type] [Key Feature/Size] [Condition] [Rarity/Edition]
```

### Examples by Category

**Streetwear/Apparel:**
```
Supreme Box Logo Hoodie Red XL DS NWT
Stüssy Classic Stock T-Shirt Black Medium New
The North Face Mountain Jacket Yellow Size L Used Good
```

**Collectibles/Trading Cards:**
```
1999 Pokemon Charizard Base Set Holographic PSA 8
Vintage Magic the Gathering Black Lotus Alpha Near Mint
2020 LeBron James Contenders Rookie Auto Graded 9
```

**Vintage/Accessories:**
```
Rolex Submariner Steel Watch 1970s Vintage Running
Vintage Levi's 501 Denim 1980s W32 L32 Rare Blue
Hermes Silk Scarf Classic Orange New
```

### SEO Keyword Placement Strategy
1. **Primary keyword** (most important): First 3-5 words
2. **Secondary keyword** (supporting): Middle section
3. **Long-tail qualifier**: Last 2-3 words
4. **Modifiers** (condition, rarity): End of title

**Example breakdown:**
```
Supreme [primary] Box Logo [secondary] Hoodie [item type] Red [color] XL [size] DS NWT [condition + rarity]
├─ Primary: Supreme
├─ Secondary: Box Logo
├─ Item Type: Hoodie
├─ Attribute: Red
├─ Size: XL
└─ Condition: DS (Deadstock) NWT (New With Tags)
```

### Character Limits & Best Practices

| Platform | Limit | Display | Notes |
|----------|-------|---------|-------|
| Shopify | 255 chars | Full in store | Use all 255 for SEO |
| Google Shopping | 150 chars | Full | Keep important info here |
| Mobile Search | 50-60 chars | Truncated | Lead with strongest keyword |
| Instagram Tags | Unlimited | 30 tags max | Use 25-30 relevant hashtags |

### Character Limit Rules
- **Minimum:** 30 characters (too short = penalized by search)
- **Ideal:** 50-70 characters for search results
- **Maximum:** 255 characters (use all space for SEO)
- **Sweet spot:** 70-120 characters (descriptive + keyword-rich)

### Banned Phrases (Avoid)
- ❌ "Amazing Deal"
- ❌ "Must See"
- ❌ "Best Price"
- ❌ "Act Now"
- ❌ All caps (appears spammy)
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks (!!!!)
- ❌ Excessive symbols (@@@, ###)

### Title Optimization Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 5 words
- [ ] Item type clearly stated (Hoodie, T-Shirt, Watch, etc.)
- [ ] Size/color included if applicable
- [ ] Condition status (DS, New, Used, Vintage)
- [ ] Rarity indicator (Limited, First Edition, Rare, OG)
- [ ] 70-120 characters optimally
- [ ] No banned phrases
- [ ] No all-caps words (except brand acronyms)
- [ ] No special characters (except hyphen, ampersand)
- [ ] Mobile readable (first 50 chars compelling)

## 3. Product Image Strategy

### Why Images Drive Sales
- **72%** of purchase decisions influenced by product images
- **80%** of visitors do NOT read descriptions before viewing images
- **Mobile:** First image is the #1 determinant of click-through
- **Conversion impact:** Proper image sequence = 20-40% higher conversion

### Recommended Image Sequence & Order

The first 5 images drive 90% of user attention. Sequence matters.

**Ideal Sequence (for apparel/streetwear):**
1. **Image 1 - Hero Shot** (center, front-facing, flat lay or worn)
2. **Image 2 - Detail Shot** (logo/branding detail close-up)
3. **Image 3 - Full View** (entire piece, all angles if possible)
4. **Image 4 - Worn/Context** (on person or styled shot)
5. **Image 5 - Back/Side** (back view or side profile)
6. **Image 6 - Size/Scale** (next to reference object for scale)
7. **Image 7 - Tag/Authentication** (tag close-up, any certificates)
8. **Image 8+ - Additional Details** (seams, material, wear areas)

**Ideal Sequence (for watches/jewelry):**
1. **Image 1 - Face On** (straight-on view of dial)
2. **Image 2 - Side Profile** (shows thickness and bezel)
3. **Image 3 - Case Back** (back view, serial numbers)
4. **Image 4 - Close-up** (bracelet detail or dial detail)
5. **Image 5 - Worn** (on wrist or modeled)
6. **Image 6 - Scale Reference** (with measuring tool or next to object)
7. **Image 7 - Packaging/Condition** (original box, papers if included)
8. **Image 8 - Authenticity** (hallmarks, maker's marks, serial numbers)

**Ideal Sequence (for collectibles):**
1. **Image 1 - Front** (main collectible face-on)
2. **Image 2 - Grading Label** (PSA, BGS, etc. if graded)
3. **Image 3 - Back/Reverse** (back view)
4. **Image 4 - Close-up** (condition/defects visible)
5. **Image 5 - Edge/Side** (shows centering for cards, age for vintage)
6. **Image 6 - In Context** (with comparison or scale)
7. **Image 7+ - Alternate Angles** (additional details)

### Total Image Count Recommendation
- **Minimum:** 4 images (not enough; 25% lower conversion)
- **Optimal:** 8-10 images (sweet spot for conversion)
- **Maximum:** 15+ images (anything beyond 10 has diminishing returns)
- **By Product Type:**
  - Apparel: 8-10 images
  - Watches/Jewelry: 10-12 images
  - Collectibles: 6-8 images
  - Vintage/Home: 10-12 images

### Image Optimization (Technical)

#### Image Size & Format
| Spec | Recommendation | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| Width | 1000-2000px | Shopify optimal: 2000px max |
| Height | 1000-2000px | Square or portrait better than landscape |
| Aspect Ratio | 1:1 or 4:5 (portrait) | Mobile-optimized |
| File Format | WebP or JPEG | WebP 30% smaller, full browser support |
| File Size | <500KB per image | Under 1MB ideal |
| DPI | 72 DPI (web) | 300 DPI not needed for digital |

#### Image Optimization Process
1. **Shoot/Source** - High-res RAW or 4K if possible
2. **Crop** - 1:1 ratio or 4:5 portrait
3. **Edit** - Consistent lighting, white balance, saturation
4. **Compress** - Reduce to <500KB without visible quality loss
5. **Name** - Descriptive filename for SEO
6. **Add Alt-Text** - For accessibility and SEO

#### File Naming Convention
```
[Brand]-[ItemType]-[Color]-[View]-[Size].webp

Examples:
- Supreme-Hoodie-Red-Front-Main.webp
- Supreme-Hoodie-Red-Detail-Logo.webp
- Supreme-Hoodie-Red-Back-Full.webp
- Rolex-Submariner-Steel-Face-Dial.webp
- Pokemon-Charizard-Front-Card.webp
```

### Alt-Text Strategy (for SEO & Accessibility)

Purpose: Help blind users AND improve SEO rankings

**Alt-Text Template:**
```
[Brand] [Item Type] [Color/Style] [View Description]

Examples:
- "Supreme Box Logo Hoodie Red Front View"
- "Rolex Submariner Steel Watch Face Dial Close-up"
- "1999 Pokemon Charizard Holographic Card Front"
- "Vintage Levi's 501 Denim 1980s Back View"
```

**Alt-Text Optimization Rules:**
- Keep under 125 characters
- Use primary keywords naturally
- Describe what's visible in image
- Don't keyword stuff (natural reading)
- Use hyphens between keywords
- No "image of" or "picture of" (implied)

### Mobile Image Optimization

Mobile represents 60-70% of traffic for most stores.

**Mobile Optimization Checklist:**
- [ ] First image is compelling at small size
- [ ] Main product visible without scrolling
- [ ] Text on images readable at mobile size
- [ ] Zoom functionality enabled (2-5x magnification)
- [ ] Images load under 3 seconds on 4G
- [ ] Portrait orientation (taller than wide)
- [ ] No watermarks (blocks purchase visibility)

### Zoom Functionality Setup

**Shopify Native Zoom:**
1. Shopify theme includes native zoom for most themes
2. Double-tap image on mobile = zoom
3. Hover magnifying glass on desktop
4. Ensure images are high resolution (2000px+ for zoom)

**Enabling in Theme:**
- Settings > Theme Settings > Product Images
- Enable "Enable image zoom"
- Enable "Enable product lightbox"

### Image Production Guide

**Lighting Setup:**
- White background (seamless paper roll best)
- 2-3 soft light sources (ring lights work great)
- Consistent color temperature (5000K daylight)
- Shadow reduction with reflectors

**Equipment Recommendation:**
- Camera: DSLR or mirrorless (or high-end smartphone)
- Lens: 50-100mm for product shots
- Lighting: Ring light ($30-200) or LED panels ($100-300)
- Background: White seamless paper roll ($20)
- Tripod: Essential for consistent framing

**Cost-Effective Alternative:**
- Smartphone with tripod
- Ring light ($50)
- White background
- Diffuser ($20)
- Total: ~$100 setup

## 4. Collection Organization

### Why Collections Matter

**SEO Benefits:**
- Collections create topical authority signals
- Each collection = potential landing page (estimated 2-5% of traffic)
- Collections naturally link-build (more backlinks = authority)
- Filter functionality improves UX and time-on-site

**Conversion Benefits:**
- Guide buyers to related items (cross-sells)
- Reduce decision paralysis ("where do I start?")
- Create scarcity (limited collections)
- Enable time-limited promotions

### Proposed Collection Structure

#### Primary Collections (Main Navigation)
These appear in main menu and drive majority of traffic.

1. **Supreme (Brand Focus)**
   - Featured Products: 12-20 hero pieces
   - Tagline: "The Supreme Collection - Authentic Vintage & Current"
   - Description: ~200 words on brand history, rarity, investment value
   - Subcategories (if using collection groups):
     - Hoodies & Sweatshirts
     - T-Shirts
     - Accessories

2. **Skateboard (Category Focus)**
   - Featured Products: 15-25 pieces
   - Tagline: "Skateboard Decks, Gear & Culture"
   - Description: Covers brands, rarity, condition standards
   - Subcategories:
     - Decks (Antihero, Chocolate, Powell Peralta, etc.)
     - Wheels & Bearings
     - Apparel

3. **Accessories (Catch-all)**
   - Featured Products: 10-15 accessories
   - Tagline: "Vintage Streetwear Accessories & Collectibles"
   - Description: What qualifies as accessories, condition standards
   - Subcategories:
     - Hats & Caps
     - Bags & Totes
     - Jewelry & Watches

4. **New Arrivals (Time-based)**
   - Featured Products: Latest 20-30 items
   - Tagline: "Just Added - Fresh Inventory"
   - Description: "Constantly updated with new additions"
   - Auto-updates (newest products appear first)
   - Refresh frequency: Daily/weekly

5. **Sale & Clearance (Promotion)**
   - Featured Products: Discounted items only
   - Tagline: "Limited Time Discounts - Up to 50% Off"
   - Description: Sale terms, end date, conditions
   - Use for: End-of-season, bulk clearance, overstock
   - Urgency triggers: "Sale ends [DATE]"

6. **Investment Grade (Special)**
   - Featured Products: Museum-quality pieces only (8.5+/10)
   - Tagline: "Collector's Edition - Investment-Grade Pieces"
   - Description: What defines investment grade, appreciation data
   - Price range: $500-5000+
   - Barrier: Educates customers on premium tier

7. **Vintage Rare Finds (Discovery)**
   - Featured Products: Rarest items (1-5 in existence)
   - Tagline: "One-of-a-Kind Vintage Finds"
   - Description: Scarcity data, authentication details
   - Rotation: Changes frequently (keeps page fresh)
   - Purpose: Drives traffic, generates buzz

#### Secondary Collections (Manual/Themed)
These serve specific marketing campaigns or customer segments.

- **Reseller Bundles** - Multi-item packages with discount
- **Birthday Gifts** - Curated for different price points
- **Corporate/Wholesale** - Bulk orders, enterprise pricing
- **Brand Spotlight** - Rotating brand features (monthly)
- **Condition Tiers** - DS/NM only, General Use, Collectible
- **Price Tiers** - Under $100, $100-500, $500-1000, $1000+

## 5. Price Display Optimization

### Why Pricing Psychology Matters

**Impact on Conversion:**
- 60% of customers make buying decisions based on price
- Price presentation affects perceived value by ±30%
- Correct positioning = higher perceived quality at same price
- Wrong positioning = price-sensitive buyers only (race to bottom)

### Psychological Pricing Strategies

#### 1. Charm Pricing (Ending in 9 or 7)
**Psychology:** Prices ending in 9 appear more discounted

```
Instead of: $500.00
Use: $499.99 (3% price reduction, 15%+ perceived discount)

Instead of: $199.00
Use: $179.99 or $189.99 (signals bargain hunting)
```

**When to Use:**
- Discounted items (sales)
- Competitive price points
- Want to signal "deal"

**When NOT to Use:**
- Premium/luxury items ($500+)
- Investment-grade pieces
- Where you want to position as "premium"

**Strategy by Tier:**
```
Budget Tier ($25-100): Always use .99 (Supreme T-shirts)
Mid Tier ($100-500): Mix .99 and .00 (hoodies, accessories)
Premium Tier ($500+): Use .00 (investment-grade pieces)
Luxury Tier ($1000+): Use .00 or .95 (rare collectibles)
```

#### 2. Anchoring (Price Comparison)

**Psychology:** First price seen becomes reference point

```
Display Original Price → Crossed Out
Then Show: Sale Price (signals savings)

Example:
Retail: $250.00 ← (crossed out, gray)
Sale Price: $149.99 ← (large, red, bold)
[Savings: $100.01 | 40% Off]
```

**Impact:** 
- Perceived savings: $100 savings = 40% off perception
- Actual savings: Only 40% off
- Conversion boost: 25-35% higher with comparison

**Implementation in Shopify:**
1. Create "Compare at Price" field (shown as strikethrough)
2. Display current price below
3. Calculate discount % automatically
4. Show urgency ("Sale ends Friday")

#### 3. Bundle Pricing (Value Stacking)

**Psychology:** Bundled prices feel like better deal

**Bundle Example 1: Apparel Bundle**
```
Supreme Hoodie (Red) - $180
Supreme T-Shirt (Blue) - $60
Supreme Cap (Black) - $40
= Individual Total: $280

Bundle Price: $199.99
Savings: $80.01 (29% off!)
```

**Bundle Example 2: Collectible Bundle**
```
Pokemon Card 1 (PSA 8) - $250
Pokemon Card 2 (PSA 7) - $150
Pokemon Card 3 (PSA 8) - $200
= Individual Total: $600

Bundle Price: $399.99
Savings: $200.01 (33% off!)
```

**Bundling Strategy:**
- Bundle slow-moving with fast-moving items
- Bundle complementary products
- 20-35% discount on bundles (perceived value)
- Create themed bundles (Supreme Essentials, Starter Collection)
- Rotate bundles monthly (freshness)

**Shopify Implementation:**
- Use apps: Bundler, Smart Bundles, Product Bundles
- Or manual: Create product bundle variant
- Show savings prominently

#### 4. Tiered Pricing (Quantity Discount)

**Psychology:** More you buy, better the deal

```
Single: $150.00
Buy 3: $135.00 each ($15 savings)
Buy 5+: $125.00 each ($25 savings)
```

**Useful for:**
- Resellers/wholesale buyers
- High-volume customers
- Clearance/inventory movement
- Building bulk purchases

**Shopify Implementation:**
- Settings > Products > Variants
- Add quantity-based pricing tier
- Or use Volume Discount app

### Sale & Discount Display Strategy

#### Sale Label Placement
```
┌─────────────────────┐
│   [SALE 40% OFF]    │  ← Top right corner (standard)
├─────────────────────┤
│                     │
│   Product Image     │
│                     │
└─────────────────────┘
```

**Label Best Practices:**
- Top-right corner (proven highest click-through)
- Red or orange badge (contrasts with product)
- Bold, clear text ("40% OFF" not "SAVE $50")
- Size: 10-15% of product image
- No transparency (solid color for visibility)

#### Discount Percentage vs. Dollar Amount
- **Under $100:** Show percentage ("40% OFF")
- **$100-500:** Show both ("$50 OFF / 20%")
- **$500+:** Show dollar amount ("SAVE $200")

#### Primary CTA: "Add to Cart"

```

✓ "Add to Cart"
✓ "Claim This Piece"
✓ "Secure Your Copy"
✗ "Purchase"
✗ "Buy"
✗ "Add"

Font Weight: Bold
Padding: 15-20px vertical, 40-50px horizontal
Corner Radius: Slightly rounded (8-12px) feels modern
Hover State: Darker shade, slight lift effect (engagement)
```

**Purpose:** Skip cart for faster checkout (especially mobile)

```
Button Style: Outlined or secondary color
```

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Q: How often should I update product descriptions?
**A:** 
- New products: Immediately (before publish)
- Existing products: Review quarterly
- Seasonal updates: Before peak season
- Refresh old products: When sales slow (give them new descriptions)

### Q: Should I use keywords or natural language in descriptions?
**A:** Both. Write naturally first, then sprinkle keywords. Google penalizes keyword stuffing. Humans first, SEO second.

### Q: How do I know if my images are good enough?
**A:** Ask: "Can I see the product clearly?" "Do I see a defect?" "Can I imagine wearing/using it?" If yes to all three, good quality.

### Q: What's the ideal number of collections?
**A:** 
- Small store (1-50 products): 3-4 collections
- Medium store (50-500 products): 5-7 collections
- Large store (500+ products): 8-15 collections
- More than 15 feels overwhelming to customers

### Q: Can I bulk edit collections?
**A:** Yes. Products > Select All > Edit Products > Add to Collection. Or use collection rules (auto-assign based on criteria).

### Q: How do I measure CRO impact?
**A:**
1. Set baseline (current conversion rate)
2. Make one change at a time
3. Wait 1-2 weeks (collect data)
4. Compare new rate to baseline
5. If +5% or higher = significant improvement

### Q: Should I use countdown timers?
**A:** Yes, but only for real sales. Fake timers hurt trust. Only use when there's a real end date.

### Q: What's the best time to run a sale?
**A:** 
- Seasonal: Before/after holiday
- Quarterly: End of season
- Flash: 24-48 hours (urgency)
- Clearance: When inventory needs movement
