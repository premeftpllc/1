## PART 2: MARGIN ANALYSIS BY PLATFORM

### Margin Calculation Methodology

**Formula:**
```
Gross Margin % = (Selling Price - Cost of Goods) / Selling Price × 100

Margin After Fees = ((Selling Price - Fees) - COGS) / (Selling Price - Fees) × 100

COGS Estimation Tiers:
- Item price > $400: COGS ≈ 50-60% of MSRP (bulk sourcing discount)
- Item price $150-400: COGS ≈ 55-65% of MSRP
- Item price $50-150: COGS ≈ 60-70% of MSRP (smaller bulk discount)
- Item price < $50: COGS ≈ 70-80% of MSRP (minimal volume discount)

Example: Supreme hoodie MSRP $168
- Sourced from StockX at: $385 (already secondary market, not MSRP)
- PremeFTP sale price: $445 (target platform)
- Platform fee: 10% (Depop)
- Calculation: (445 - 44.50 - 385) / (445 - 44.50) = 15.6% net margin
```

### Detailed Seasonal Pricing Strategy

**Q1 (Jan-Mar): Post-Holiday Clearance**
- Pricing: 15-25% markdown on slow movers from Q4
- Inventory: Clear archive stock; focus on new spring pickups
- Tactics: Flash sales (24-48hr windows) to move inventory
- Margin: Accept 18-22% (lower) to improve cash flow

**Q2 (Apr-Jun): Spring/Early Summer Stabilization**
- Pricing: Return to normal (25-35% margin target)
- Inventory: Focus on summer-appropriate items (lighter tees, shorts)
- Tactics: Bundle/cross-sell strategies; "summer starter" collections
- Margin: 25-30% (steady state)

**Q3 (Jul-Sept): Peak Selling Season**
- Pricing: Premium pricing (+5-10% above Q2)
  - Summer stock running thin (scarcity premium)
  - Back-to-school/fall collection buzz building
  - New fall releases command hype premium
- Tactics: "Limited availability" messaging; collection updates 2x/week
- Margin: 30-35% (highest of year)

**Q4 (Oct-Dec): Holiday + New Year**
- Pricing: Two-phase
  - **Oct-Nov:** +5-10% premium (holiday gift buying)
  - **Dec 1-24:** +10-15% premium (last-minute scarcity)
  - **Dec 26-Jan 15:** -20-30% markdown (clearance push)
- Tactics: Gift guides, holiday bundles, "perfect for gift-giving" messaging
- Margin: 28-32% (Oct-Nov); 18-22% (post-holiday)
