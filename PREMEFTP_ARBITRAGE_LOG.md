## Daily Transaction Log

### Format Guide
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD format
- **Source**: StockX, eBay, Amazon, Other
- **Item**: Product name & size/variant
- **Cost**: Acquisition price (including fees)
- **List Price**: Published sale price on Shopify
- **Profit**: List Price - Cost - Fulfillment Costs
- **Status**: Sourced, Listed, Sold, Shipped, Completed
- **Notes**: Relevant details, issues, or flags

## Threshold Rules & Guidelines

### Minimum Profit Requirements
- **Sneakers/Footwear**: $50+ minimum
- **Electronics**: $75+ minimum (higher fulfillment costs)
- **Luxury Goods**: $100+ minimum (requires premium handling)
- **Collectibles/Sports**: $40+ minimum

### Source-Specific Rules

**StockX:**
- Scan below-retail filter minimum 1x daily (target 2x)
- Exclude items with pending authentication
- Check return window (14 days standard)
- Validate size/variant accuracy before acquisition

**eBay:**
- Focus on auction-end bargains and "Best Offer" negotiations
- Verify seller ratings (minimum 98%+ positive)
- Check for bulk lots opportunitites
- Luxury goods priority (higher margin potential)

**Amazon:**
- Monitor flash sales and lightning deals
- Focus on electronics with high review counts (4.5+)
- Bulk purchase advantages (2+ units)
- Watch for overstock liquidations

### Profit Threshold Overrides
- Items below minimum are escalated to supervisor for approval
- Acceptable scenarios: Bulk purchases with blended margin, high-velocity categories, test inventory
- Reject all acquisitions below $35 profit (absolute floor)

## Status Definitions

| Status | Definition | Next Action |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Sourced** | Item purchased & acquired | Process inventory receipt; QC check; upload product data |
| **Listed** | Item published on Shopify storefront | Monitor for inquiries; adjust pricing if needed; track impressions |
| **Sold** | Purchase order received from customer | Generate shipping label; notify fulfillment team; expedite packaging |
| **Shipped** | Package dispatched to customer | Send tracking confirmation; monitor delivery status |
| **Completed** | Delivery confirmed; post-sale follow-up sent | Request feedback; archive transaction; close profit log entry |
| **Liquidated** | Below-threshold item sold at cost/loss | Document reason; analyze root cause; update sourcing filters |
| **Cancelled** | Order cancelled or item returned | Refund processing; item returned to inventory; reassess listing strategy |
